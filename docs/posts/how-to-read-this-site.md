This site answers one question once a month: what do the best-known investing frameworks say about conditions right now?

## Start with the stance

The dashboard opens with a single word: Overweight, Neutral or Underweight. It tells you whether the frameworks, taken together, lean toward holding more stock than usual, less, or neither.

Most months it will say Neutral. That is by design. The frameworks only move the stance when most of them agree, two months in a row.

## Then look at who voted how

Below the stance, the frameworks are grouped into families.

- **Drivers** vote on direction: the economy's regime, Fed policy, market trend, and sentiment.
- **Alarms** watch for recession and credit stress. They can only subtract. A quiet alarm is the absence of bad news, not a reason to buy.
- **Valuation** never moves the stance. It limits how large a move can be.
- **Where to look** shows which asset classes and sectors are leading. It is context, not part of the score.

Tap any framework to open its lesson.

## Use the lessons

Every framework has a page covering the idea, where it came from, how it is calculated here, how to read it, how investors use it, and where it has failed. The last part matters most. No framework works all the time, and knowing how each one breaks is what keeps you from leaning on it too hard.

## Try the calculator

Enter your portfolio and the mix you would hold with no view at all. The calculator shows what this month's readings imply, in percentages and dollars.

## Read the backtest with care

The backtest page shows what following each framework would have returned since the early 2000s. The short version: most of them did not beat simply holding stocks. What many of them did was lose less in the worst stretches. The page opens with its own limits. Read those first.

## What this site is not

It is not a forecast, and it is not personal advice. It does not know your goals, your taxes or your time horizon. It is a way to learn the frameworks professionals talk about, and to see what they are saying this month.
